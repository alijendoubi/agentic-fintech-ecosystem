## 1. Classification Assessment

### Current Classification: Limited Risk

Algorithmic trading AI is **not** classified as High Risk under Annex III of the EU AI Act (as of May 2026). The system operates as a **proprietary trading system** — it does not provide advice to retail investors, does not make automated decisions affecting natural persons' rights, and is not listed in Annex III.

**Annex III categories reviewed and excluded:**

| Annex III Category | Applicable? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Biometric identification | No | System does not identify natural persons |
| 2. Critical infrastructure | No | Proprietary trading, not infrastructure management |
| 3. Education | No | Not an education system |
| 4. Employment & worker management | No | Not used for employment decisions |
| 5. Essential services (credit scoring, etc.) | No | Proprietary trading, not consumer credit |
| 6. Law enforcement | No | Not applicable |
| 7. Migration / border control | No | Not applicable |
| 8. Justice / democratic processes | No | Not applicable |

**ESMA Annual Review Risk:** ESMA explicitly notes that AI in algorithmic trading may be reclassified annually. This document is reviewed each year.

## 2. Current Obligations (Limited Risk)

### Transparency Obligations (Article 50)

The system interacts with human operators via the Zone C HITL Interface. When presenting trade signals or override requests, the system must:

- **Disclose AI involvement:** Operators are informed that trade signals are generated by an AI system (the Cognitive Core)
- **Not impersonate humans:** The HITL interface clearly labels all AI-generated content
- **Deepfake disclosure:** N/A (system does not generate synthetic content for human consumption)

Implementation: HITL Interface displays "AI-generated signal" label on all trade proposals.

### GPAI / Foundation Model Obligations

The system uses foundation models from Anthropic and Mistral AI. Per Article 53+, these providers must comply with GPAI obligations (effective August 2025). Steps taken:

- [ ] Requested GPAI technical documentation from Anthropic (via Bedrock support)
- [ ] Requested GPAI technical documentation from Mistral AI
- [ ] Confirmed no training on customer data in contracts (TP-001, TP-003, TP-004)
